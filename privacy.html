Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Andrey AI is a private backup tool used only by its owner.

What data the app accesses

The app uses the Google Drive permission "drive.file". With it the app can see, create, change and delete only the files that the app itself has created in the owner's Google Drive. It cannot read any other file, and it does not access email, contacts or any other Google data.

How the data is used

The app uploads encrypted backup files to the owner's Google Drive and removes old backup files after a retention period. That is its only purpose.

Sharing and storage

No data is shared with, sold to or transferred to anyone. The app has no servers of its own that collect user data. The Google login token is stored only on the owner's server.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
